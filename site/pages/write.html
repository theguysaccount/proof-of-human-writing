Write with a replay

Write here and the editor records how the text comes together. When you're done, you get a replay file to send to whoever asked how you wrote it.

You have a draft in this browser

Starting a new piece deletes this draft and its recording from this browser. If you want to keep it, continue the draft and download its replay first.

What gets recorded

  • Every change to the text: what was added or removed, and where.
  • When each change happened, rounded to a twentieth of a second.
  • How the browser says it arrived: a key press, a paste, a drop, undo, or something else.

What doesn't

  • Which keys you hold and for how long, your mouse, your screen, or other tabs and apps.
  • Anything about you or your device. There is no account and no cookie.

The recording stays in this browser until you download it. A replay contains your text and the rhythm of your typing, which is personal in the way handwriting is. Share it the way you'd share a draft: with people you choose.

A replay shows how a text was typed. It doesn't prove who typed it, and your reader is told that. What a replay can and can't show
