What a replay can and can't tell you

  • It shows how a text was entered into the Writing Replay editor: each key press, pause, deletion and paste, in order.
  • It can't show who was at the keyboard, or what was on their other screens. Text can be retyped from another source.
  • It isn't tamper-proof. A program can type like a person, and a replay file can be fabricated by someone with the skill.

Treat a replay as evidence about process, to go with a conversation with the writer. How to read a replay
